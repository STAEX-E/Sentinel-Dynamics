The Sentinel Model

Why this business model works for defense.

A single, tightly-integrated pipeline — rather than a scattered vendor network — is what makes rapid, sovereign UAV development possible.

01

Vertical Integration

Design, fabrication, avionics and testing sit under one roof — cutting procurement delay and communication loss between vendors.

02

Indigenous Supply Chain

Reduced reliance on imported components strengthens strategic autonomy and shortens lead times during conflict-critical windows.

03

Modular Product Family

A shared platform architecture across interceptors, surveillance and VTOL airframes lowers cost-per-unit and simplifies maintenance.

04

Scalable Production

A manufacturing approach built to scale from prototype runs to fleet-level output without re-architecting the supply chain.
